1. Accepting these terms

By using the FlashPay app or website you agree to these terms. They are an agreement between you and ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED, which operates FlashPay. If you do not agree, please do not use FlashPay. When we make important changes, we will tell you in the app.

2. Who can use FlashPay

  • You must be at least 18 years old
  • You must use FlashPay in Nigeria with a valid Nigerian phone number
  • You must give true and complete information when you register

If we find the information is false, we may suspend or close the account.

3. Your account

  • Keep your PIN secret. FlashPay will never ask for your PIN, password or one-time code
  • You are responsible for what happens on your account
  • Tell us at once if you think someone else has accessed it
  • Keep your contact details up to date
  • Each person may hold one personal account

4. What you can do with FlashPay

  • Fund your wallet
  • Send money to Nigerian bank accounts
  • Buy airtime and data
  • Pay electricity bills and TV subscriptions

We will add new features over time. Any new feature may have its own terms, which we will show you before you use it.

5. What you must not do

  • Use FlashPay for fraud, money laundering or any illegal activity
  • Use someone else's account or details
  • Try to break, bypass or overload our systems, including with bots or scripts
  • Give false information at registration or during verification
  • Harm other users

If you break these rules we may suspend your account and report the matter to the authorities.

6. Payments and fees

All amounts are in Nigerian naira. Most payments are quick, but banks and partners can cause delays.

When you confirm a payment with your PIN, it is final and you cannot reverse it yourself. If you send money to the wrong account we cannot promise to recover it, though we will help you ask the receiving bank.

We will show you any fee before you confirm a payment. If a payment fails after money is taken, we will return it to your wallet. If it does not return, contact us with the payment reference.

  • Losses because you shared your PIN or let someone use your phone

  • Payments you confirmed to the wrong recipient
  • Interruptions for maintenance or other reasons beyond our control

To the extent the law allows, our liability for any claim is limited to the value of the payment in dispute, and we are not liable for indirect losses.

8. Suspending or closing an account

We may suspend or close an account if you break these terms, if we see suspicious activity, if you give false information, or if the law requires it. You can ask us to close your account at any time. We will arrange for any balance left in your wallet to be returned to a bank account in your name.
