1. Accepting these terms

By using the FlashPay app or website you agree to these terms. They are an agreement between you and ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED, which operates FlashPay. If you do not agree, please do not use FlashPay. When we make important changes, we will tell you in the app.

2. Who can use FlashPay

  • You must be at least 18 years old
  • You must use FlashPay in Nigeria with a valid Nigerian phone number
  • You must give true and complete information when you register

If we find the information is false, we may suspend or close the account.

3. Your account

  • Keep your PIN secret. FlashPay will never ask for your PIN, password or one-time code
  • You are responsible for what happens on your account
  • Tell us at once if you think someone else has accessed it
  • Keep your contact details up to date
  • Each person may hold one personal account

4. What you can do with FlashPay

  • Fund your wallet
  • Send money to Nigerian bank accounts
  • Buy airtime and data
  • Pay electricity bills and TV subscriptions
  • Save money in savings plans
  • Earn FlashPoints and use them for cashback
  • Ask another FlashPay user to pay you

We will add new features over time. Any new feature may have its own terms, which we will show you before you use it.

5. What you must not do

  • Use FlashPay for fraud, money laundering or any illegal activity
  • Use someone else's account or details
  • Try to break, bypass or overload our systems, including with bots or scripts
  • Give false information at registration or during verification
  • Harm other users

If you break these rules we may suspend your account and report the matter to the authorities.

6. Payments and fees

All amounts are in Nigerian naira. Most payments are quick, but banks and partners can cause delays.

When you confirm a payment with your PIN, it is final and you cannot reverse it yourself. If you send money to the wrong account we cannot promise to recover it, though we will help you ask the receiving bank.

We will show you any fee before you confirm a payment. If a payment fails after money is taken, we will return it to your wallet. If it does not return, contact us with the payment reference.

7. Savings

FlashPay lets you save money from your wallet in savings plans.

  • Flexible plans, such as goal and emergency plans: you can add money and take it out whenever you like
  • Locked plans: you choose an unlock date, and the money cannot be taken out before that date. Please choose the date carefully, because we do not release locked money early
  • AutoSave: you choose an amount and how often (daily, weekly or monthly), and we move that amount from your wallet to your plan automatically. If your wallet does not have enough money, we skip that saving and tell you. After 3 missed savings in a row, AutoSave pauses. You can pause, resume, change or stop it at any time

Savings plans do not earn interest, and we do not promise any return on your savings. A savings plan is not a bank account. You must take the money out of a plan before you delete it.

8. FlashPoints and cashback

FlashPoints are rewards we give for using FlashPay and for inviting friends. You can use them to get cashback in your wallet, as the app shows.

  • FlashPoints are not money. You cannot sell them, transfer them or take them out as cash
  • We decide how points are earned and used, and we may change the rules or end the rewards. We will tell you in the app about important changes
  • A referral reward is given only after your friend makes a qualifying payment, as the app shows
  • We may remove points that were earned through fraud, false referrals or misuse

9. Request money

You can ask another FlashPay user to pay you by sending a payment request.

  • The other person can pay, decline or ignore a request. A request is not a debt and is not a promise to pay
  • A request can expire. You can cancel it, and the other person can block requests from you
  • Do not use requests to harass, deceive or pressure anyone. We may limit or remove this feature from an account that is misused
  • When someone pays a request, the payment follows the rules in section 6 and is final

10. Limits and account protection

  • We set limits on how much you can send or pay, to protect you and to follow the law. You can see your limits in the app. We may change limits, and we may ask you to verify your identity (for example with your BVN or NIN) before raising them
  • You can set a lower limit for large payments in your settings
  • Several wrong PIN attempts in a row lock your account for a while
  • We may ask for extra confirmation, hold or decline a payment that looks unusual, or restrict an account for a short time while we check it
  • If you lose your phone or think someone knows your PIN, change your PIN at once and tell us

11. Our responsibility

We are not responsible for:

  • Losses because you shared your PIN or let someone use your phone
  • Delays or failures at banks, networks or partners outside our control
  • Payments you confirmed to the wrong recipient
  • Interruptions for maintenance or other reasons beyond our control

To the extent the law allows, our liability for any claim is limited to the value of the payment in dispute, and we are not liable for indirect losses.

12. Suspending or closing an account

We may suspend or close an account if you break these terms, if we see suspicious activity, if you give false information, or if the law requires it. You can delete your account in the app under Profile, Settings, Delete account. First move or withdraw any money left in your wallet and savings, and we will ask for your PIN. If you cannot do this, contact us and we will help return the balance to a bank account in your name. We may have to keep some records for as long as the law requires.

13. Our property

The FlashPay name, logo, app and website belong to ESSENCE BUSINESS PLATFORM LIMITED. You may not copy, change or distribute them without written permission, or try to extract the source code of the app.

14. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria and disputes go to the Nigerian courts. Please contact us first, because most problems can be solved quickly without going to court.
