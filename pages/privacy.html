Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Draft before launch. FlashPay has not opened to the public yet. This page explains how we plan to handle your data and will be reviewed and finalised before launch. Last updated: October 2026.

1. What we collect

We collect what we need to run your account and keep it safe.

What you give us:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your 6-digit PIN, which we store in a protected form and never as readable text
  • Identity details such as BVN, NIN or an ID document, where verification is required

What we record as you use the app:

  • Your payments, with amounts, dates, recipients and status
  • Device details and sign-in records, which we use to protect your account
  • A notification token, so we can send you payment alerts

We do not collect your phone contacts or your messages. If we offer fingerprint sign-in, your fingerprint stays on your phone and is never sent to us.

2. How we use it

  • To create and run your account
  • To process transfers, airtime, data, electricity and TV payments
  • To confirm who you are and to prevent fraud
  • To send payment alerts, security notices and account updates
  • To help you when you contact support
  • To fix problems and improve the app
  • To meet legal and regulatory duties in Nigeria

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

We share data only with the companies that help us run FlashPay, and only what they need:

  • Payment and bill partners: VTpass, Flutterwave and Monnify
  • Hosting and database providers: Supabase and Vercel
  • Email delivery: Resend

We may also share data when the law requires it, when a lawful request comes from the authorities, or to protect FlashPay and its users from fraud. Each partner has its own privacy policy.

4. How we protect it

Your PIN is stored in a protected form, connections to our servers are encrypted, and access to customer data is restricted. Automated checks look for unusual activity. Read more on our Security page.

No system is completely safe, so please choose a PIN that is hard to guess and never share it.

5. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act you can ask to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, to object to how we use it, to withdraw consent, and to ask us to delete it. We will reply within a reasonable time. Send your request on WhatsApp.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is open. Financial rules in Nigeria may require us to keep payment records for several years after an account closes. When we no longer need data, we delete it or remove anything that identifies you. We will state our exact retention periods before launch.

7. Website and cookies

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It loads its font from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address when you visit.

8. Children

FlashPay is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think a child has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as FlashPay grows or the law changes. We will change the date at the top and tell you in the app about important changes.

10. Contact us

Message us on WhatsApp or see the Contact page.
